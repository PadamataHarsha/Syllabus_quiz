Introduction to Data Science

Data science combines statistics, programming, and domain knowledge to analyze information.

A data pipeline is the process of collecting, cleaning, transforming, and storing data.

A dataset is a collection of observations or records used for analysis.

A model is a mathematical representation used to make predictions or explain patterns.

Data quality includes accuracy, completeness, consistency, and relevance.

Ethical data use includes protecting privacy, avoiding bias, and using data responsibly.
